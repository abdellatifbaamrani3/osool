# أصول · OSOOL — Brand Summary

## Brand name

**أصول (OSOOL)** — Arabic for *origins / roots*.

Kept from the current store on purpose: it already carries the exact idea the
brand sells — going back to the root cause instead of treating symptoms — and it
reads as credible in both Arabic and Latin. English descriptor evolves from
"for hair & scalp" to a health-house frame so the gummies line fits under the
same roof.

- **Arabic:** أصول
- **Latin:** OSOOL
- **Descriptor (new):** العلم قبل الوعد · Science before the promise
- **Tagline (kept):** العناية تبدأ من الأصل — *care begins at the root*

## Identity

A Saudi science-led supplement & haircare house that **behaves like a
pharmacy, not like an ad.** Calm authority, clinical clarity, zero hype.

Personality: the knowledgeable pharmacist who tells you the truth even when it
doesn't sell — names the active, writes the dose, states where the evidence
ends, and points you to a doctor when that's the honest answer.

Voice: Saudi dialect, plain and direct. Confident but never loud. No
countdown timers, no "miracle," no fake scarcity. Trust is built, not demanded.

### Identity pillars

1. **Named actives, written doses** — the concentration is on the box, by the number.
2. **Honest limits** — we say when it works, when it won't, and when to see a doctor.
3. **Pharmacy-grade standards** — COA available on request; manufacturing and
   regulatory standards stated plainly (see "claims to earn" below).
4. **Made for Saudi conditions** — heat, covered scalp, hard water — not a translated European product.
5. **Evidence with its footnotes** — we cite the study *and* its limitation.

## Branding & positioning

**Positioning statement:**
For the label-reader who is tired of vague promises, أصول is the Saudi
health-house that treats supplements and haircare with pharmacy-grade honesty —
named ingredients, written concentrations, and openly stated limits — so you
choose with a clear head instead of on hype.

- **Category:** science-led supplements & haircare (KSA), pharmacy-grade positioning
- **ICP:** 25–45, reads ingredient labels, has been burned by "natural extract"
  products, wants clinical proof but in plain Arabic; skews women (postpartum,
  post-diet) but inclusive of men.
- **Enemy:** vague marketing — "خلاصات طبيعية" with no dose, before/after theatre, hype.
- **Proof of promise:** written concentrations, COA on request, stated study
  limits, 30-day money-back, human WhatsApp support.

## Color palette

Clinical + premium. Green anchors trust and health (and reads well in KSA);
gold stays a thin premium accent, not the star; lots of paper-white for the
clean pharmacy feel.

sections; gold reserved for a single accent per section; Verified Green only for
trust/confirmation cues.

## Claims to earn before publishing them

These are written into the site as *pathway/on-request* language only. Upgrade
to concrete claims once you hold the documents:

- SFDA registration numbers (cosmetic e-cosma / supplement GHAD)
- GMP certificate of the manufacturer
- A named pharmacist / formulator on the team
- Certificate of Analysis (COA) per batch — currently promised "on request"
- Real, verified customer reviews (current ones are labelled placeholders)
